# V4.0 Market Intelligence

V4.0 separates model strength from market price and adds closing-line analysis.

## Metrics
- Market implied probability
- Model-vs-market edge
- Expected value (EV)
- Open-to-current movement
- Closing Line Value (CLV)

CLV is calculated from entry and closing prices. A positive value means the entry price implied a lower probability than the closing price, i.e. the bettor obtained a better price than the close.

## Discipline
- CLV is an evaluation metric, not proof of future profitability.
- A price move is not automatically classified as "sharp money".
- Missing entry/closing prices means CLV is unavailable.
- No market price means no EV claim and therefore no forced BET.
